# The Big Project Deliverable 2: Project Development Checkpoint

## Overview

The purpose of this deliverable is to evaluate your team's progress from project planning to project implementation.

Since Deliverable 1, your team should have started transforming your proposed idea into a working data science workflow. This deliverable focuses on demonstrating that your team has begun implementing your project, working with real-world engineering data, and generating preliminary results.

Through this milestone, your team should demonstrate the ability to:

- Translate a project proposal into an initial implementation.
- Work with real-world engineering datasets and address practical data challenges.
- Apply appropriate data science methods to investigate engineering questions.
- Organize your project so that others can understand, reproduce, and evaluate your work.
- Reflect on your progress and identify the remaining steps required to complete the project.

This deliverable is **not expected to represent a finished project**. However, your team should provide evidence that meaningful progress has been made and that your project direction is feasible for completion.

This deliverable should answer:

- What parts of your proposed project have been implemented?
- How has your team processed and analyzed the available data?
- What preliminary insights or results have been obtained?
- Can another person understand and reproduce your current workflow?
- What remains to be completed before the final project demonstration?

## 4. Project Documentation and Reproducibility
